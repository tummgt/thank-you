Your Semester Goal

A short guide to choosing one personal goal for this semester and making a plan for it. It uses WOOP, a method developed by psychologist Gabriele Oettingen. Your goal can be about anything: friends, a hobby, sport, everyday life or your studies.

Find a quiet moment. Go through the four steps in order and take a few seconds at each step to really imagine it. Keep each answer to a few words.

  1. W

    Wish Step 1

    What is one wish you would like to fulfil this semester? Choose something that is challenging but realistic for you.

  2. O

    Outcome Step 2

    What would be the best result of fulfilling your wish? How would you feel? Picture it as vividly as you can.

  3. O

    Obstacle Step 3

    What is the main thing in you that might stop you? Think of a habit, a feeling or a thought, not outside circumstances. Picture it, too.

  4. P

    Plan Step 4

    What can you do to overcome your obstacle? Put it into one sentence:

    If [obstacle], then I will [action].

Your answers stay in this browser only. Nobody else, including the research team, can see them.

Another example

W
Cook a proper dinner with my flatmates once a week.
O
Our flat feels like a home and we get to know each other better.
O
I put it off because planning feels like effort.
P
If I catch myself putting it off, then I will suggest one dish in our flat chat on Monday.

Tip: repeat WOOP whenever you start a new goal. A free version with more guidance is available at woopmylife.org.
