Your thank-you · Winter semester 2026/27

Free Munich for Students

Concerts, art, markets and good spots in Munich that cost nothing (or one euro). Thank you for taking part in our study.

Information as of October 2026. Times and programmes change, so check the linked website before you go.

Live music 3 tips

  • Concerts at the University of Music and Theatre (HMTM)

    Students and professors perform almost every week, from orchestra to opera scenes. Most events have free admission; the calendar marks them “Eintritt frei”.

    Arcisstraße 12 · Gasteig HP8 · other venues
    Mostly free
  • JazzJam@Milla

    Monthly jam session of the HMTM jazz department. Listen, or bring your instrument.

    Milla Live Club, Holzstraße 28 · Tuesdays, doors 20:00
    Free
  • Glockenspiel at Marienplatz

    The figures on the New Town Hall dance and joust for about 15 minutes.

    Marienplatz · daily 11:00 and 12:00
    Free

Art and museums 6 tips

  • Lothringer 13 Halle

    The city’s hall for contemporary art in Haidhausen, with changing exhibitions and events.

    Lothringer Straße 13
    Free
  • NS-Documentation Centre

    Munich’s history in the Nazi era, on the site of the former party headquarters.

    Max-Mannheimer-Platz 1
    Free
  • Palaeontological Museum

    Fossils and skeletons in a historic hall, a short walk from the Pinakotheken.

    Richard-Wagner-Straße 10
    Free
  • Pinakotheken, Brandhorst and more on Sundays

    Alte Pinakothek, Pinakothek der Moderne, Museum Brandhorst, Glyptothek, Egyptian Museum, Museum Five Continents and others.

    Kunstareal and city centre · every Sunday
    €1
  • Lenbachhaus after work

    The Blue Rider collection, free on the first Thursday of the month in the evening.

    Luisenstraße 33 · 1st Thursday, 18:00–22:00
    Free
  • Haus der Kunst and Villa Stuck

    Haus der Kunst is free on the last Friday of the month (16:00–22:00); Villa Stuck on the first Friday (18:00–22:00).

    Prinzregentenstraße 1 and 60
    Free

Markets and festivals 2 tips

  • Kirchweihdult

    Munich’s traditional autumn fair with about 250 stalls: crockery, antiques, crafts and a few old-fashioned rides.

    Mariahilfplatz · 17–25 Oct 2026, 10:00–19:00
    Free entry
  • Tollwood Winter Festival

    Winter festival with the “Market of Ideas”, live music and performances, open late into the evening. Over 90% of the cultural programme costs nothing.

    Theresienwiese · 24 Nov – 23 Dec 2026
    Free entry

Outdoors 4 tips

  • Monopteros in the English Garden

    Small temple on a hill with a view over the old town’s towers. Best in the late afternoon.

    Englischer Garten, southern part
    Free
  • Olympiaberg

    A short climb with a view across the city and, on clear days with Föhn wind, to the Alps.

    Olympiapark · U3 Olympiazentrum
    Free
  • Sunset at the Isar

    Sit on the gravel banks between Wittelsbacherbrücke and the Flaucher.

    Isarauen, Sendling / Thalkirchen
    Free
  • Nymphenburg Palace Park

    Canals, swans and long avenues. The park itself costs nothing; only the palace buildings charge entry.

    Schloss Nymphenburg · Tram 17
    Free

A place to read 1 tip

  • Bavarian State Library

    Register once for a free library card, then work in the reading rooms of one of Europe’s largest libraries. Look up at the grand staircase on your way in.

    Ludwigstraße 16 · U3/U6 Universität
    Free card
